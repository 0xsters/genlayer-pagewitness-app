Ask about a page

Paste a public page and ask one question about what it says: a deadline, an eligibility rule, a price, a requirement. The answer is stored only after validators agree, together with a verbatim quote from the page.

Questions

Answering a question fetches the live page. A validator agrees only when its own reading reaches the same outcome, the quote is found word for word in its own copy of the page, and the two answers state the same fact.

PageWitness is a witnessed-answer primitive for program rules, documentation claims, deadlines, and prices: instead of arguing over screenshots, both sides can point at one on-chain answer and the exact quote it rests on.
